"Jobs" shows the work Palitra does in the background: connector syncs, CSV processing, webhook
intake, attribution recomputes, dashboard refreshes and the background tool calls the agent starts
from chat. It tells you what is running, what finished and why a job failed — and lets you retry
a failed job.

Every project member sees the job list. Project managers, and the organization's owner and
admins, can retry, cancel and delete jobs; other members see no actions in the table.

## Open the section

1. Pick the project in the project list in the sidebar.
2. Click "Jobs" in the sidebar, or press ⌘ J on a Mac (Ctrl J on Windows and Linux).

When the agent starts a background job in chat, its message shows a card with the job's status.
Click the card to open "Jobs" with that job shown above the list (see "Open a job from chat"
below).

## What the table shows

Jobs are listed newest first. The columns:

| Column      | Contents                                                                    |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Type"      | The kind of job, as a system name such as `etl_sync` (see the table below). |
| "Connector" | The connector the job belongs to, or "—".                                   |
| "Status"    | The current status; a failed job has an icon with the reason next to it.    |
| "Progress"  | A bar and a percentage, when the job reports them.                          |
| "Started"   | When the job started, such as "5 minutes ago"; hover for the exact time.    |
| "Duration"  | How long the job ran; for a running job it keeps counting.                  |
| "Retries"   | How many times the job was retried, such as "×2".                           |
| "Actions"   | The "…" menu with the job's actions.                                        |

Statuses update on their own; there's no need to reload the page. Below the table are the page
number and the "Previous" and "Next" buttons.

Job types you may see in the "Type" column:

| Type                    | What it is                                                                                 |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `etl_sync`              | A connector sync: loading data from an ad account or an analytics service.                 |
| `csv_ingestion`         | Processing an uploaded CSV file.                                                           |
| `webhook_batch_ingest`  | Taking in a batch of data that arrived through a webhook.                                  |
| `attribution_recompute` | Recomputing attribution.                                                                   |
| `connector_reprocess`   | Rebuilding events from a connector's already loaded data after its event mapping is saved. |
| `tool_run`              | A background call of a connector tool that the agent started from chat.                    |
| `artifact_refresh`      | An on-demand refresh of a dashboard artifact.                                              |
| `reconciliation_sync`   | A data reconciliation from older Palitra versions. No new ones are created.                |
| `workflow_…`            | A step of a [workflow](/workflows/).                                                       |

## Filter jobs by status

1. Open the status list above the table; it shows "All statuses" by default.
2. Pick a status, such as "Failed". To see everything again, pick "All statuses".

The filter resets when you switch to another project.

## Job statuses

| Status            | Meaning                                                                                     |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Pending"         | The job is queued and hasn't started yet.                                                   |
| "Running"         | The job is in progress.                                                                     |
| "Cancelling…"     | A cancel was requested; the job stops at its next safe point.                               |
| "Completed"       | The job finished successfully.                                                              |
| "Failed"          | The job failed. The reason is in the tooltip of the icon next to the status.                |
| "Cancelled"       | The job was cancelled.                                                                      |
| "Postponed"       | The platform's API quota stopped the sync; it carries on later.                             |
| "Outcome unknown" | A background tool call may have changed data on the platform, but its result wasn't stored. |

**"Postponed".** The clock icon's tooltip says: "The platform's API quota ran out. The next sync
runs from … and carries on from here." Nothing to do: the connector's next sync is a new job and
picks up where this one stopped.

**"Outcome unknown".** The tooltip says: "The call may have changed data on the platform. Check
there before retrying." Open the ad account and make sure the change didn't go through before you
run it again.

## Find out why a job failed

Hover over the red icon next to the "Failed" status. The tooltip has two lines: what happened and
what to do. Common reasons:

| What happened                                                             | What to do                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| "The connector's authorization has expired."                              | "Reconnect the credential in the connector settings."   |
| "The account has no access to the requested data."                        | "Ask an account owner to grant access, then retry."     |
| "The connector is disabled, so the sync did not start."                   | "Re-enable the connector before retrying."              |
| "Another sync of this connector was running, so this one did not start."  | "Wait for that sync to finish."                         |
| "The platform kept returning a temporary error, and the retries ran out." | "Retry the job."                                        |
| "The platform refused the request."                                       | "This error cannot be retried — open a support ticket." |
| "The connector's saved event mapping is no longer valid."                 | "Open the connector's event mapping and save it again." |
| "The job ran past its time limit."                                        | "Retry it, or narrow what it was asked to do."          |
| "The tool is blocked for this project."                                   | "Allow it in the connector's tool permissions."         |

The error text may carry a detail such as "Platform code: …" or "Table: …". The full, untrimmed
error text is shown on the job's card when you open it from chat.

## Open a job from chat

When the agent starts a background job, chat shows a card with the job type and status
("Pending", "Running", "Completed" and so on). Click it:

1. "Jobs" opens with this job's card above the list.
2. The card shows the status, "Connector", "Started", "Finished" and, for a postponed sync,
   "Resumes". For a failed job, the full error text follows.
3. To go back to the plain list, click "Show all jobs".

The card shows the job even when it isn't on the list's current page or doesn't match the
selected filter. If the job was deleted, the card says "Job not found".

## Retry a job

You can retry a job whose status is "Failed" or "Cancelled".

1. On the job's row, click "…" in the "Actions" column.
2. Choose "Retry".

The job goes back to "Pending" and the "Retries" count goes up by one. The job is retried in
place; no new row appears.

<Screenshot id="jobs-actions-menu" alt="A job's actions menu with Retry, Cancel and Delete" />

"Retry" is missing when the error is marked as non-retryable ("The platform refused the
request."). For a workflow step it is replaced by "Retry in Workflow", which takes you to
[Workflows](/workflows/): steps are retried from the workflow run. A job older than 30 days can't
be retried.

If the retry doesn't go through, Palitra tells you why:

- "A sync is already running for this connector. Try again once it finishes."
- "Only failed or cancelled jobs can be retried." — the job's status has already changed.
- "Retry is unavailable because the task payload was not preserved."
- "A write call can't be retried. Run it again from chat." — a background call that changes data
  on the platform is only repeated by asking the agent again.
- "Too many background calls are running. Try again later."
- "Couldn’t reach the task broker. We’ll keep this job pending — try again if it doesn’t pick up
  shortly." — the job stays "Pending".
- "You don’t have permission to retry this job."

## Cancel a job

You can cancel a job that is "Pending" or "Running".

1. On the job's row, click "…".
2. Choose "Cancel".

A queued job is cancelled at once and becomes "Cancelled". A running job first shows
"Cancelling…" and stops at its next safe point.

"Cancel" isn't offered for workflow steps (they are cancelled with the workflow run) or for a
running background call that changes data on the platform: stopping it halfway isn't safe.

## Delete a job

Only a finished job can be deleted: "Completed", "Failed", "Cancelled" or "Postponed".

1. On the job's row, click "…" and choose "Delete".
2. In "Delete this job?", click "Delete". "Keep" closes the dialog.

The job and its stored result are removed for good.

## Clear finished jobs

To delete many old jobs at once:

1. Click "Clear finished" above the table.
2. In "Clear finished jobs?", tick the "Statuses" to delete: "Completed", "Failed", "Cancelled",
   "Postponed". All are ticked by default.
3. Under "Finished", pick how old: "Any time", "More than a day ago", "More than 7 days ago" or
   "More than 30 days ago".
4. Click "Delete".

Palitra reports how many jobs were deleted, such as "12 jobs deleted". Running and pending jobs
are never deleted. Only people who can manage jobs see the "Clear finished" button.

## If something doesn't load

- "Live updates paused" — the live connection was lost, so statuses refresh periodically and may
  lag. Click "Reconnect". If the message asks you to sign in again, reload the page or sign in.
- "Couldn’t load jobs" — click "Retry".
- "No jobs yet" — no background work has run in the project yet. Jobs appear once work starts,
  for example a connector sync, a data upload or a background call from chat.
