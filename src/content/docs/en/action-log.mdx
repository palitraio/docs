"Action Logs" is the history of changes in the project's ad accounts. It records:

- actions the agent took in connected ad accounts from [chat](/chat/) or in a
  [workflow](/workflows/): changing bids and budgets, starting and stopping campaigns, editing
  ads, keywords and other settings;
- changes made in the ad accounts outside Palitra — by you, your colleagues or the ad platform
  itself.

Each record shows what changed, where, when and where the change came from, and after a while
whether it helped: Palitra compares metrics before and after.

Project managers and viewers, and the organization's owner and admins, see the log. Guests
don't have access to it. Records are read-only: they can't be edited or deleted.

## Open the log

1. Pick the project in the project list at the top of the sidebar.
2. Click "Action Logs".

<Screenshot id="action-log-cards" alt="Action Logs shown as cards, with filters above the list" />

Until there are records, you see "No actions recorded yet".

## What is recorded

### Agent actions

A record appears when the agent has carried out an action that changes something in an ad
account. Answering questions and reading data are not recorded. The source of such a record is
"Chat" or "Workflow".

### External changes

Palitra finds changes made directly in the ad accounts on its own: after each sync it compares
campaign settings with the previous sync. This is done for Yandex.Direct and VK Ads accounts.

- Changes are collected per day: one record is one campaign, one kind of change (strategy,
  budget, bids, campaign status, ad texts and creatives) on one day. That is why an external
  record has a date but no time.
- Changes Palitra made itself are left out: they are already recorded as agent actions. A change
  reverted on the same day isn't recorded either.
- Changes the platform made automatically are marked "automatically by the platform". Their
  effect isn't measured.
- Archiving and deleting objects aren't recorded as external changes.

External changes appear only after campaigns sync. If you pick "External changes" in the filter
and there are none, the log shows "No external changes found" — "They appear after campaigns
sync.".

## Cards and table

At the top right of the list are two icons: a card list and a table. The log opens as cards.

A card shows:

- a coloured dot on the left — the action's outcome (same colours as the outcome badges, see
  [below](#outcome-of-an-action));
- the action's name, such as "Bid changed" or "Campaign stopped", and the date;
- for an external change, a short description of what changed;
- the source: "Chat", "Workflow" or an "External change" badge;
- the campaign, by name and number; click it to show only that campaign's records;
- the object, when the action touched an ad group, ad or keyword rather than the campaign
  itself;
- the apply mode: "Direct" or "Experiment";
- the outcome: the primary metric and its change, "Evaluating" or "Effect not measured".

The table shows the same data in the "Date", "Action", "Campaign", "Apply mode", "Source" and
"Outcome" columns.

Records go from newest to oldest, 20 per page. With more than one page, page controls appear
under the list.

## Filter records

Above the list are three filters. They can be combined.

| Filter         | Values                                                                               |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "All outcomes" | "Positive", "Negative", "Neutral", "Confounded", "Evaluating", "Effect not measured" |
| "All sources"  | "Chat", "Workflow", "External changes"                                               |
| "All modes"    | "Direct", "Experiment"                                                               |

To clear a filter, pick "All outcomes", "All sources" or "All modes" in it again. If nothing
matches, the log shows "No actions match your filters".

### One campaign's records

1. Click the campaign's name on a card, in a table row or in a record's details.
2. A "Campaign: …" badge appears above the list, and the log keeps only that campaign's records.
3. To show all campaigns again, click the cross on that badge.

The chosen campaign is kept in the page address: send the link to a colleague and it opens the
log for that campaign.

## Record details

Click a card or a table row and the "Action details" panel opens on the right.

<Screenshot
  id="action-log-details"
  alt="The Action details panel with the Result section and the Before and After metrics table"
/>

The top of the panel shows the action's name, the campaign, the changed object, the connector,
the date and the source. An action from chat has an "Open conversation" link to the chat where
the agent took it.

Below that, depending on the record:

- **"What changed"** — for external changes. A table with "Object", "Setting", "Before" and
  "After" columns. Long values, such as ad texts, are clipped: click "Show in full" to see all
  of it and "Collapse" to hide it again.
- **"Result"** — the effect assessment, see [below](#outcome-of-an-action).
- **"Technical details"** — for agent actions, collapsed by default: the "Tool" the agent
  called, the "Apply mode", the "Experiment" with its status (when the action ran as an
  experiment), the "Workflow run" (when a workflow took the action), the "Connector ID" and the
  call's "Parameters".

## Apply mode

| Mode         | Meaning                                                 |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Direct"     | The change was applied in the ad account straight away. |
| "Experiment" | The change runs as an experiment on the ad platform.    |

An experiment's status shows in the technical details: "Draft", "Running", "Concluded" or
"Abandoned".

## Outcome of an action

After an action Palitra waits for an observation window, then compares metrics before and
after. For "Direct" changes the window is one week for bid changes and two weeks for other
actions; an experiment is scored from the experiment's result on the platform. Until it is
scored, the record's outcome is "Evaluating".

The "Result" section shows:

- the verdict and an explanation;
- "Primary metric: …" — the metric the action was judged by and how it moved;
- a "Metric", "Before", "After", "Change" table. Next to a change you see "better" or "worse"
  for metrics with a clear direction: lower is better for CPA, higher for conversions;
- the compared periods: "Before: …", "After: …" and their length in weeks.

The verdicts in the record details and the values of the "All outcomes" filter are worded a
little differently:

| Verdict in the details | Filter value          | Meaning                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Improved"             | "Positive"            | The primary metric moved in the right direction.                                                                    |
| "Worsened"             | "Negative"            | The primary metric moved in the wrong direction.                                                                    |
| "No notable effect"    | "Neutral"             | The primary metric barely moved.                                                                                    |
| "Inconclusive"         | "Confounded"          | Other changes or anomalies hit the object during the observation window, so this action's effect can't be isolated. |
| "Evaluating"           | "Evaluating"          | The observation window is still running.                                                                            |
| "Effect not measured"  | "Effect not measured" | The effect of this change isn't measured.                                                                           |

"Effect not measured" is set right away when there is nothing to compare with: for example, the
project has no accumulated statistics yet, or the platform made the change automatically.

"Could not be scored" means there wasn't enough data at the end of the observation window to
score the effect.

## Errors

- "Failed to load action logs" — click "Retry".
- "Failed to load record" — in the details panel; click "Retry".
