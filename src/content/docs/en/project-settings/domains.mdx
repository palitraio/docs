Published [artifacts](/artifacts/) open on the Palitra address by default. On the "Domains" tab
you can connect your own domain, such as `reports.example.com`, and publish artifacts there.

Every project member sees the tab. Project managers, and the organization's owner and admins,
can add, check and delete domains.

## Open the tab

1. Pick the project in the project list at the top of the sidebar.
2. Click the gear icon next to the project name ("Project settings").
3. Go to the "Domains" tab.

<Screenshot
  id="project-settings-domains"
  alt="The Domains tab in project settings with the list of connected domains"
/>

If you see "Custom domains aren't available" instead of the list, the feature is off on your
Palitra installation: artifacts are published on the Palitra address only.

## Connect a domain

1. Click "Add domain".
2. In "Add a custom domain", enter the "Hostname" artifacts should open on, such as
   `reports.example.com`.
3. Click "Add domain".

The domain appears in the list as "Pending", with its DNS records table open below it.

<Screenshot
  id="project-settings-domains-add"
  alt="The Add a custom domain dialog with the Hostname field"
/>

If the domain is already connected in Palitra, you get an error saying so. If the hostname is
malformed, Palitra asks you to check it and try again.

## Create the DNS records

Create the records at your DNS provider, wherever you manage the domain. To see them in Palitra,
click "Show DNS records" on the domain's row. The table has "Type", "Name" and "Value" columns,
with buttons to copy the name and the value.

Palitra asks for:

| Type                                        | Purpose                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `TXT` named `_palitra-verify.<your domain>` | Proves the domain is yours.                                              |
| `CNAME` to the Palitra address              | Points a subdomain (such as `reports.example.com`) at Palitra.           |
| `A` to the Palitra addresses                | Used instead of `CNAME` when you connect an apex domain (`example.com`). |

Copy the values from the table, not from this article: they differ for every domain.

If the table says "A records could not be listed right now. The table below may be incomplete.",
click "Retry".

## Check the domain

Once the records exist, click "Check now". DNS changes take time to spread: if the check fails,
wait and try again.

When the check passes, the status changes to "Active" and the row shows when the domain was
verified and "Certificate valid until …". Palitra issues the HTTPS certificate itself.

If the DNS server doesn't answer, Palitra shows "DNS did not answer; nothing changed." and the
domain keeps its status.

## Statuses and errors

| Status    | Meaning                                                   |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Pending" | The domain is added but its records are not verified yet. |
| "Active"  | The domain works and artifacts can be published on it.    |
| "Broken"  | The check failed or the certificate couldn't be issued.   |

Under a "Broken" domain Palitra gives the reason:

- "The verification TXT record wasn't found." — check the `TXT` record: name and value must match
  the table.
- "The domain doesn't point to the Palitra address." — check the `CNAME` or `A` records.
- "The TLS certificate couldn't be issued." — usually caused by wrong DNS records; fix them and
  click "Check now".
- "The certificate couldn't be renewed and is about to expire." — check that the DNS records are
  still in place.
- "The DNS server didn't answer." — check again later.

Next to the certificate date you may see "Expires soon" (less than two weeks left) or "Expired".

## Artifacts on the domain

The domain's row lists "Artifacts on this domain". Click a name to open the artifact.

An artifact restricted to project members can't be published on a custom domain: publish it
publicly or on the Palitra address.

## Delete a domain

1. Click "Delete" on the domain's row.
2. Confirm with "Delete domain".

Publications on this domain fall back to the Palitra address. You can then remove the DNS
records at your provider.
