The "Members" tab shows who works in the project and with which role. It is also where people
are added, invited and removed.

Every project member sees the member list. Project managers, and the organization's owner and
admins, can add people, change roles, remove members and manage invitations. Only the owner and
admins can invite someone who isn't in the organization yet as a manager or viewer.

## Open the tab

1. Pick the project in the project list at the top of the sidebar.
2. Click the gear icon next to the project name ("Project settings").
3. Go to the "Members" tab.

<Screenshot id="project-settings-members" alt="The Members tab with the project's members and their roles" />

The member table has "Member", "Email", "Role" and "Added" columns.

## Project roles

| Role | Who | What they can do in project settings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Manager" | An organization member who runs the project. | Changes project settings and manages members. |
| "Viewer" | An organization member who needs to see the project. | Sees settings but can't change them. |
| "Guest" | Someone invited by email, possibly from outside your organization. Reaches only this project. | Sees settings but can't change them. |

The organization's owner and admins can change any project as a manager would, even when they
are not in the member list.

Each person has one role in a project, set separately for each project: the same person can be
a manager in one project and a viewer in another.

## Add managers and viewers

Managers and viewers are picked from organization members.

1. Click "Add members".
2. In "Add project members", pick a "Role": "Manager" or "Viewer".
3. In "Who to add", start typing a name or email and pick the person from the list. You can pick
   several people; remove one with its cross.
4. Click "Invite".

Organization members join the project right away, without an email.

<Screenshot id="project-settings-members-add" alt="The Add project members dialog with the role picker and the list of organization members" />

When the list is empty, Palitra says why: "Everyone in the organization is already in the
project or picked." or "No matches." — with a hint on inviting someone new if you are allowed to.

### Invite someone new to the organization and the project

The organization's owner and admins can invite a person who isn't in the organization yet:

1. In "Add project members", pick the "Manager" or "Viewer" role.
2. In "Who to add", type the full email address.
3. Click the "Invite … to the organization and this project" row that appears.
4. Click "Invite".

The person gets one invitation. Accepting it joins them to both the organization and this
project with the chosen role. Such invitations are listed under "Organization invitations
granting this project".

A project manager who isn't the organization's owner or an admin sees the hint "Managers and
viewers are picked from organization members." Invite the person as a guest instead, or ask the
owner or an admin to add them to the organization.

## Invite a guest

1. Click "Add members".
2. Pick "Role" → "Guest".
3. In "Emails", type an address and click "Add" (or press Enter). Repeat for each guest.
4. Click "Invite".

A guest receives an email invitation and reaches only this project. If someone with that
address already has an account in your organization, Palitra adds them to the project right
away.

The invitation is valid for 7 days. Until it is accepted, it is listed under "Pending project
invitations".

## Results and errors

After you click "Invite", Palitra reports how many people were added and how many were invited.
If someone couldn't be added, the message lists the address and the reason:

- "Already a member of this project."
- "This address already has a pending invitation — resend it from the invitations list instead."
- "A new user can only be given the guest role." — the person has no Palitra account; invite them
  as a guest, or have the owner or an admin invite them to the organization.
- "This person belongs to another organization — invite them as a project guest instead."
- "The address or the role is not acceptable."
- "This project no longer exists. Refresh the page."

If nobody could be added, you see "Could not add any members".

If you haven't confirmed your own email yet, Palitra won't send invitations and shows "Confirm
your email address first — check your inbox for the verification link, or request a new one from
the verification screen."

## Manage invitations

Below the member list, managers see "Pending project invitations": invitations nobody has
accepted yet. The "Expires" column shows how long each stays valid. Each invitation has these
actions:

- "Copy invite link" — to send the invitation yourself, for example in a messenger;
- "Resend invitation" — sends the email again;
- "Revoke" — the invitation link stops working.

The organization's owner and admins also see "Organization invitations granting this project":
people invited to the organization and this project at once. The same actions are there, but
"Revoke" asks for confirmation and cancels the whole organization invitation, not just access to
this project.

## Change a member's role

1. On the member's row, click the "…" icon → "Change role".
2. In "Change project role", pick the new "Role".
3. Click "Save".

Palitra confirms with "Role updated".

## Remove a member

1. On the member's row, click the "…" icon → "Remove from project".
2. Confirm with "Remove".

The person loses access to the project and Palitra confirms with "Member removed". The
project's last manager can't be removed: Palitra shows "Could not remove member". Make someone
else a manager first.

## Leave a project

If you are a member of the project, the tab has a "Leave project" button.

1. Click "Leave project".
2. Confirm with "Leave".

You lose access to the project and can be re-invited later. The last manager can't leave:
Palitra shows "You are the last manager — promote another member to manager first."

The organization's owner and admins who aren't in the member list don't see this button.
