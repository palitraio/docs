The "Members" tab shows who belongs to the organization and with which role, and it is where
you invite new people. Below the table is the list of invitations that haven't been accepted
yet.

The organization has three roles:

- "Owner" — runs the whole organization: billing, single sign-on, deleting the organization.
- "Admin" — "Manages members, projects and AI settings".
- "Member" — "Access is limited to assigned projects".

Roles are summarized in [For owners and admins](/getting-started/owner-admin/). Here is what each
role can do on this tab:

| Action | Owner | Admin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invite members, revoke or resend an invitation | yes | yes |
| Remove a member from the organization | yes | yes |
| Change a member's role | yes | no |
| Transfer ownership | yes | no |
| Grant or revoke SSO bypass | yes | no |

The owner's row has no actions menu: the owner can't be removed or demoted from this tab.

## Open the tab

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose "Organization settings".
2. Go to the "Members" tab.

The members table has the columns "Member", "Email", "Role", "Joined" and "Actions".

<Screenshot id="organization-members" alt="The Members tab with the members table and the invitations list" />

## Invite a member

1. Click "Invite members".
2. In the "Invite to organization" dialog, enter the person's "Email".
3. Choose the "Organization role": "Admin" or "Member".
4. If you chose "Member", the "Project access" block appears. Tick the projects and pick a role
   for each: "Manager", "Viewer" or "Guest". The block is shown only when the organization has
   at least one active project. For the "Admin" role the block isn't shown.
5. Click "Send invitation".

You see "Invitation sent", and the invitation appears in the pending invitations list. The
person receives an email with a link. An invitation is valid for 7 days.

Project roles are explained in [For project managers](/getting-started/manager/).

<Screenshot id="organization-members-invite" alt="The Invite to organization dialog with the role and project access options" />

### If the invitation isn't sent

Palitra shows "Could not send invitation" with the reason:

- "This address already has a pending invitation — resend it from the invitations list
  instead."
- "This person is already a member of the organization."
- "This person already belongs to another organization. A person can belong to exactly one
  organization — invite them as a project guest instead." See
  [Project members](/project-settings/members/) for how.
- "One of the selected projects no longer exists. Reopen the dialog and pick the projects
  again."
- "Confirm your email address first — check your inbox for the verification link, or request a
  new one from the verification screen." You can invite people only after confirming your own
  email.

### If SSO is enforced in the organization

The invite dialog shows a block "This organization uses SSO (…)". Invited people will sign in
through your identity provider. Send them the sign-in address: you can copy it on the "Single
Sign-On" tab. See [Single Sign-On](/organization/sso/).

## Manage invitations

The owner and admins see the pending invitations list. It has the columns "Email", "Role",
"Expires" and "Actions". The actions menu of an invitation offers:

- "Copy invite link" — copy the invitation link to send it yourself, for example in a
  messenger. You see "Invite link copied".
- "Resend invitation" — send the email again. You see "Invitation resent".
- "Revoke" — cancel the invitation. Confirm in the "Revoke invitation for …?" dialog with
  "Revoke". The link from the email stops working.

With no invitations, the list reads "No pending invitations".

<Screenshot id="organization-members-invitations" alt="The pending invitations list with an invitation's actions menu" />

## Change a member's role

Only the owner can change roles.

1. Open the actions menu in the member's row and choose "Change role".
2. In the "Change role" dialog, pick the "Role": "Admin" or "Member".
3. Click "Save". You see "Role updated".

You can't change your own role. The only way to make someone else the owner is to transfer
ownership.

If a role claim is set in the single sign-on settings, the member's role is taken from the role
mapping again at their next SSO sign-in. See [Single Sign-On](/organization/sso/).

## Transfer ownership

To make another member the owner:

1. Open the actions menu in the future owner's row and choose "Transfer ownership".
2. Read the warning: the member "becomes the owner and you become an admin. You lose access to
   billing, SSO settings and deleting the organization. Only the new owner can transfer it
   back."
3. Click "Transfer". You see "Ownership transferred".

## Remove a member

1. Open the actions menu in the member's row and choose "Remove from organization".
2. Confirm with "Remove". The person loses access to all projects in this organization.

You see "Member removed".

## Leave the organization

Admins see "Leave organization" in their own row. Once you confirm, you lose access
to all projects in this organization; you can be re-invited later.

The owner doesn't have this item: to leave, first transfer ownership to another member.

## SSO bypass

When single sign-on is enforced, members have to sign in through the identity provider. The
owner can let a particular person sign in with a password as well — for example, an automation
account, or for emergency access if the provider is down.

1. Open the actions menu in the member's row.
2. Choose "Grant SSO bypass". To undo, choose "Revoke SSO bypass".

A member with bypass gets a "Bypass SSO" badge in the table. These items appear only when SSO is
enforced. The owner can always sign in with a password.

## Loading errors

If the table doesn't load, you see "Couldn't load members"; for the invitations list,
"Couldn't load invitations". Click "Try again".
