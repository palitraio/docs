The "General" tab is the first tab of the organization settings. Here you set the
organization's name, URL slug and type, and the owner can delete the whole organization.

Only the owner and admins see the organization settings. Both can edit the profile; the
"Danger zone" block with organization deletion is shown to the owner only. Roles are explained
in [For owners and admins](/getting-started/owner-admin/).

## Open the tab

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Choose "Organization settings".
3. The "General" tab opens.

If the menu has no "Organization settings" item, your role is "Member". Ask the organization's
owner or an admin to make the change or to change your role.

## Edit the organization profile

The "Organization profile" block has three fields:

- "Organization name" — what the organization is called in Palitra.
- "URL slug" — a short Latin name for the organization: "Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens
  only". Until you edit the slug by hand, Palitra fills it in from the name — so renaming
  the organization can change the slug too. Check this field before saving.
- "Organization type" — "Agency" ("We manage advertising for multiple clients") or "In-house"
  ("We manage advertising for our own brand").

To save your changes:

1. Edit the fields.
2. Click "Save changes". The button is active only when the form has changes.
3. A "Saved" notice appears.

If saving fails, you see "Could not save changes". This happens, for example, when another
organization already uses the slug: pick a different one.

### What the URL slug affects

The slug is part of addresses you may already have shared:

- Published [artifacts](/artifacts/) open on a subdomain carrying the organization's slug. When
  you edit the field, Palitra warns: "Published artifacts will move to the subdomain with the
  new slug; old links to them will stop working."
- The single sign-on address contains the slug: `/orgs/<slug>/login`. After changing the slug,
  share the new address with your team. See [Single Sign-On](/organization/sso/).

## Delete the organization

Only the owner can delete the organization. This is permanent: all projects, members,
invitations and AI configuration are deleted.

1. At the bottom of the "General" tab, in the "Danger zone" block, click "Delete organization".
2. In the "Delete organization" dialog, type the organization name exactly as it appears in the
   profile. The hint above the field reads: Type "…" to confirm.
3. Click "Delete organization". The button is active only when the name matches.

After deletion you see "Organization deleted", Palitra signs you out and opens the sign-in
page.

If deletion fails, you see "Could not delete organization". Try again later.

If you want to hand the organization over to someone else rather than delete it, use "Transfer
ownership" on the "Members" tab: see [Members](/organization/members/).
