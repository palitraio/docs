The "Billing" tab shows the organization's plan, how much of the plan's limits is used, which
card is on file and which invoices were issued. It is also where you change the plan, add
add-ons and configure AI model keys.

The owner and admins see the tab, with different rights:

| Action                                                             | Owner | Admin |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----- | ----- |
| View the plan, usage and invoices                                  | yes   | yes   |
| Change the plan, cancel and resume the subscription                | yes   | no    |
| Buy and turn off add-ons                                           | yes   | no    |
| Add and update the payment method                                  | yes   | no    |
| Provider keys, spending limit and custom models in "AI model keys" | yes   | yes   |
| AI billing mode and utility model                                  | yes   | no    |

Admins see the "Change plan" and "Manage add-ons" buttons but can't pick a plan or an add-on.
The "Change plan" dialog tells them: "Only the organization owner can change the plan or payment
method. Find the owner on the Members tab."

## Open the tab

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose "Organization settings".
2. Go to the "Billing" tab.

The "Upgrade plan" and "Buy add-on" buttons in limit hints elsewhere in Palitra lead here too,
opening the right dialog straight away.

## Subscription status

At the top of the tab Palitra shows a banner when the subscription needs attention:

| Banner                      | What it means and what to do                                       |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Start your free trial"     | Click "Add card to start". Nothing is charged.                     |
| "… days left in your trial" | The trial is running; below is the AI budget spent.                |
| "Trial AI budget used up"   | No AI; everything else works until the trial ends.                 |
| "Your trial has ended"      | Click "Choose a plan" to keep using Palitra.                       |
| "Payment failed"            | The last payment didn't go through. Click "Update payment method". |

While the subscription is active and paid, there is no banner. Adding a card during your first
sign-in is covered in [First steps](/getting-started/first-steps/).

## Current plan

The "Current plan" card shows the plan's name and price: "… / mo" or "… / mo, billed
annually". Below it is one of these lines:

- "Renews on …" — the next renewal date.
- "Cancels on …" — the subscription is cancelled and ends on this date.
- "Switches to annual billing on …" or "Switches to monthly billing on …" — a billing interval
  change is scheduled.
- "Active until …" — for the Enterprise plan, when the contract has an end date.

The owner and admins see the "Manage add-ons" and "Change plan" buttons.

## Usage and limits

Below the plan are cards with usage for the current period:

- "Projects used" — how many projects exist out of the plan's limit.
- "Workflow runs this month" — how many times [workflows](/workflows/) ran. If the plan doesn't
  include workflows, the card reads "Not included". "Details" opens the "Workflow runs" dialog
  broken down "By day" and "By project".
- "Token usage this month" — AI spend. With platform credits, the caption shows how much the
  plan includes. If the organization pays for AI with its own keys, the card reads "Own keys"
  and "Using your own AI keys". "Details" opens token usage by day for the same period as the
  card, with the "Projects", "Models" and "Users" tabs. Spend is attributed to whoever started
  the work: the author of a chat message, the creator of a scheduled task or monitor, the
  person who ran a workflow by hand (for a scheduled or webhook run, the workflow's creator).
  "System" is calls no one started by hand. "Not recorded" is spend whose person could not
  be determined, including spend recorded before Palitra began tracking users.
- "Wordstat units this month" — if the plan includes them. The caption says when the counter
  renews.

With no limit, the card reads "Unlimited" instead of a counter. At 80 % of a limit the bar turns
amber, at 100 % red. The "Projects used" and "Workflow runs this month" cards then show an
"Upgrade" link.

When a limit is used up, an action elsewhere in Palitra may open a dialog titled, for example,
"Project limit reached" or "Workflow run limit reached". It has the buttons "Buy add-on" (when a
matching add-on exists), "Upgrade plan" and "Maybe later". The project limit is covered in
[Projects](/organization/projects/#project-limit).

## Change the plan

Only the owner can change the plan.

1. Click "Change plan". The "Change plan" dialog opens.
2. Pick the billing interval: "Monthly" or "Annual". The annual option shows its discount.
3. Pick a plan. The current one is marked "Current". Plans that aren't self-serve show a
   "Contact sales" link instead of a price.
4. Palitra calculates the cost and shows an "Upgrade to …" or "Downgrade to …" dialog saying what
   will happen: "You'll be charged … now.", "You'll receive a … credit toward future invoices."
   or "Nothing is charged now. This change takes effect on …".
5. Click "Confirm".

Changes are prorated against your current billing period.

What can get in the way:

- "Add a payment method first" — changing plans needs a card on file. See
  [Payment method](#payment-method).
- "Reduce usage before downgrading" — the new plan has lower limits. Palitra lists what to do,
  for example "Reduce projects from … to …" or "Remove the … add-on". Archived projects don't
  count toward the limit: archive extra projects (see [Projects](/organization/projects/)) or
  turn off add-ons and try again.
- "You're already on this plan." — you picked the current plan.
- "Enterprise plans aren't self-serve — contact sales at" — for Enterprise, write to sales.

## Promo code with a first-month discount

A Palitra manager can issue a promo code for a discount on the first month, for example 50%.
It is the alternative to the free week: either a week free, or a discount on the first month.

- Enter the promo code before a trial starts, in the promo code field on this tab or at first
  sign-in (see [First steps](/getting-started/first-steps/)). If you have already had a trial,
  you see "The discount is only available before the trial starts".
- After you enter the code, the top of the tab shows "N% off your first month" with the date
  the discount is valid until. This discount comes without a free trial: adding a card does not
  start one.
- Click "Pay for the first month", choose a plan and confirm. Next to the price you see
  "… — first month": that is what is charged now. Add-ons that are already enabled are charged
  at full price.
- The discount applies to monthly billing only. Annual billing is marked "The discount doesn't
  apply to annual billing": if you choose it, you pay the full price and the discount is lost.
- From the second month the card is charged the plan's full price.
- The first month's invoice lists the discount as a separate line.
- One promo code per organization: after a discount code you cannot enter a free-days code, and
  the other way round.
- If the code expired or was revoked while you were confirming, nothing is charged: you see
  "The price has changed. Check the new amount and confirm again." with the new amount.

## Cancel or resume the subscription

1. Click "Change plan".
2. At the bottom of the dialog, click "Cancel subscription".
3. Confirm in the "Cancel subscription?" dialog with "Cancel subscription".

The plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, then it won't renew. The plan
card shows "Cancels on …".

Changed your mind? Open "Change plan" and click "Resume subscription". Without a card, Palitra
asks: "Add a payment method to resume your subscription."

## Add-ons

Add-ons extend your plan: they add projects or workflow runs, or turn on features such as single
sign-on.

1. Click "Manage add-ons". The "Add-ons" dialog opens.
2. On the add-on you need:
   - if the add-on can be bought several times over, set the quantity with "Decrease quantity"
     and "Increase quantity" and click "Purchase";
   - if it's a monthly subscription, flip the switch ("On" / "Off");
   - if it's a one-time purchase, click "Purchase".
3. Palitra shows the cost, for example "This add-on adds …/mo to your bill.". Click "Confirm".

An active add-on is marked "Active". If an add-on isn't available on your plan, it reads
"Available on … and above". Only the owner can buy add-ons, and a card on file is required:
otherwise you see "Add a payment method first".

## Payment method

The "Payment method" section shows the card on file and its expiry: "Expires …". With no card
it reads "No payment method on file.".

Only the owner can add or replace the card:

1. Click "Add payment method" or "Update".
2. Enter the card details in the secure form. "Your card details are sent directly to our
   payment provider and never touch our servers." If the form opens in a CloudPayments window, a
   1 RUB hold is placed on the card to verify it and released right away — nothing is charged.
3. Save the card.

What can go wrong:

- "We couldn't save this card. Check the details or try a different card."
- "Couldn't load the payment form. Please try again."
- "Your card was saved, but this organization already used its free trial. Choose a plan to
  continue." — click "View plans".
- "This card is on a payment provider we no longer use." — click "Update payment method" and
  add the card again.

Organizations on a contract Enterprise plan don't have the "Payment method" section: they are
invoiced instead.

## Invoices

The "Invoices" section lists the organization's invoices with the columns "Invoice", "Date",
"Amount", "Status" and "Download". Statuses: "Paid", "Pending", "Overdue", "Void".

To get the PDF, click "Download" in the invoice's row. The invoice opens in a new tab.

- "This invoice's PDF is still generating. Try again in a moment."
- "Your browser blocked the invoice tab. Allow pop-ups for this site and try again."

## AI model keys

The "Token usage this month" card has an "AI model keys" button. It opens the settings for how
the organization accesses and pays for AI models.

- "Billing mode" — "Platform credits" (AI is paid for with Palitra credits, no keys needed) or
  "Bring your own keys" (you connect your provider keys; "Palitra is not billed."). Only the
  owner switches the mode. "Bring your own keys" takes effect once you add at least one key.
- "Spending limit" — with platform credits: "Monthly limit (USD)" and "Notify at (%)". When
  spend reaches the threshold, a warning with a "Manage limit" link appears at the top of
  Palitra. When the limit is reached, AI usage is paused.
- "Provider keys" — with your own keys. Paste the "API key" and click "Test & save". Keys are
  stored encrypted. You can't delete the last key in this mode.
- "Custom models" — models with an OpenAI-compatible API (for example vLLM or Ollama). Click
  "Add model".
- "Utility model" — with your own keys: the model for background tasks such as SQL generation,
  chat titles and context compaction. Only the owner can change it.

<Screenshot
  id="organization-billing-ai-keys"
  alt="The AI model keys dialog with the billing mode choice"

If the provider stops accepting a key, it gets the "Invalid key" status: replace it with a valid
one.

## Contract Enterprise plan

If the organization is on a contract Enterprise plan, your account manager handles the plan and
add-ons. So the tab has no "Change plan" or "Manage add-ons" buttons and no "Payment method"
section. Usage and invoices are shown as usual. If there is a payment issue, the banner says:
"Contact your dedicated account manager to resolve this."

## If Palitra runs on your company's servers

In such an installation the plan comes from the license rather than a subscription, and no card
is needed for it. The AI billing mode is set by the license too: in the "AI model keys" dialog
the unavailable mode reads "Set by your license — the AI payment mode cannot be switched
here.".

If something is wrong with the license, a message appears at the top of Palitra, for example
"Your license is degraded — the workspace is temporarily read-only." or "Your license is
suspended — please contact your Palitra representative.". In these cases, contact your Palitra
representative.

## Loading errors

"Couldn't load billing" — click "Retry". For individual sections: "Couldn't load payment
method.", "Couldn't load invoices", "Couldn't load plans", "Couldn't load add-ons".
