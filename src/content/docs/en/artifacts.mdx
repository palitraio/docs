Artifacts are what the agent builds for you in chat: pages, dashboards, presentations and
documents. Each artifact is kept in the project with all its versions. You can open it, print it,
publish it by link — for everyone or for project members only — and refresh a dashboard with
fresh data on a schedule.

Every project member sees the "Artifacts" section. What else you can do depends on your role:

| Action | Who can |
| --- | --- |
| View artifacts and their versions | Every project member |
| Restore versions, refresh data by hand | Project managers and viewers, the organization's owner and admins |
| Rename, duplicate, publish, unpublish, delete, set auto-refresh and forms | Project managers, the organization's owner and admins |

## Open the section

Pick the project in the sidebar and click "Artifacts". The project's artifact list opens.

When the agent creates or changes an artifact in chat, the conversation shows a card reading
"Created · version …" or "Updated · version …". Click "Open" to view it without leaving the chat.

<Screenshot id="artifacts-list" alt="The project's artifact list with search, filters and the Name, Updated, Publication and Data columns" />

## Create an artifact

The agent creates artifacts. Ask it in [chat](/chat/) to build, say, a spend dashboard or a
promo page, and the result shows up in the list.

If the project has no artifacts yet, the section says "No artifacts yet". Click "Ask the agent":
Palitra opens a new chat with a ready-made request for a simple dashboard from the project data.

## Find an artifact in the list

The table has four columns:

- "Name" — the artifact's name and an icon for its type: "Page", "Dashboard", "Presentation" or
  "Document".
- "Updated" — when the artifact last changed.
- "Publication" — "Public", "Members", "Not published" or "Taken down". A published artifact has
  icons next to it that open its page on the Palitra address and, if it is published on a custom
  domain, on that domain.
- "Data" — for artifacts with data: when it was refreshed ("Updated …"), "Never updated", or a
  "Stale" mark. Hover over the mark to see why.

Above the table are the "Search by name" box and filters:

- "Type" — one of the four types or "All types";
- "Publication" — "Published", "Not published" or "Any status";
- "Audience" — "Public", "Members only" or "Any audience";
- sorting by "Last updated" or "Name", in "Descending" or "Ascending" order.

If nothing fits the filters, you see "Nothing matches". Click "Reset filters". When there are
many artifacts, the bottom of the list has a "Load more" button.

## Open an artifact

Click an artifact's row. The artifact panel opens on the right:

- the header shows the name, type and publication status;
- the middle shows the artifact itself;
- at the bottom are the "Versions", "Data" (artifacts with data) and "Forms" (pages) tabs.

You can copy the link to an open panel from the browser's address bar and send it to a colleague:
it opens the same artifact for them. The browser's Back button closes the panel, and so does the
"Close panel" cross.

<Screenshot id="artifacts-panel" alt="The artifact panel: header with publication status, preview, and the Versions, Data and Forms tabs" />

If the preview fails to load, you see "Preview unavailable": click "Try again".

Documents and presentations can be printed: in the "Actions" menu (three dots in the header),
choose "Print". If the browser blocks the new window, Palitra shows "Open the document to print
it" with an "Open document" link.

## Change an artifact

The agent changes an artifact's content. Open the chat the artifact was made in: in the list,
click the three dots on the artifact's row and choose "Go to chat". The item is there as long as
that chat exists. Tell the agent what to fix: every change is saved as a new version.

The agent can also delete an artifact, but only once you approve. The agent does not handle
publishing, access, domains or form destinations: you do that in the "Artifacts" section.

## Versions

The "Versions" tab lists every version of the artifact with its date and size. Marks next to the
number:

- "Current" — the latest version;
- "Published" — the version the link serves;
- "Viewing" — the version shown in the panel right now;
- "Render failed" — the version rendered with errors. While you view it, the errors are listed
  below the versions under "Render errors in version …".

To look at an older version, click "View" next to it. To bring it back, click "Restore": Palitra
saves a copy of it as the new current version, marked "Restored from version …". No history is
lost.

If a version loads anything from other websites, their addresses are listed under "External
domains".

## Rename or duplicate

**Rename.** In the panel, click the "Rename" pencil next to the name, type the new one and press
Enter (Esc cancels). In the list, use the three dots on the row → "Rename" → the "Rename
artifact" dialog → "Save". Names are up to 200 characters.

**Duplicate.** Choose "Duplicate" in the panel's "Actions" menu or the row menu. Palitra creates a
copy and shows "Copy created"; from the panel, the copy opens right away.

## Publish an artifact

A published artifact opens by link, without signing in to Palitra or for project members only,
as you choose.

1. Open the artifact.
2. In the "Actions" menu, choose "Publish…".
3. In the "Publish artifact" dialog, under "Who can open it", choose:
   - "Anyone with the link" — a public page;
   - "Project members only" — it opens only for people signed in to Palitra who are in the
     project.
4. In "Version", choose which version to publish. The current one is the default.
5. In "Address", keep "Palitra address" or pick a custom domain. Custom domains are connected in
   [project settings](/project-settings/domains/); only working domains are listed.
6. With a custom domain, fill in "Path on the domain", such as `promo`.
7. Click "Publish".

<Screenshot id="artifacts-publish" alt="The Publish artifact dialog with audience, version and address choices" />

Publishing limits:

- A members-only artifact is published on the Palitra address only: "Members-only artifacts are
  available on the Palitra address only."
- If the artifact shows project data and you publish it publicly, Palitra asks you to confirm:
  "The data will become public". Tick "I understand the data becomes public" and click "Publish
  publicly".
- The number of published artifacts in an organization is limited by the plan. When the limit is
  reached, Palitra tells you so — see [Billing](/organization/billing/).

Once published, the status in the panel header changes to "Public" or "Members only". Click it to
see the publication date and the links: "Palitra address" and, if set, "Custom domain". The
buttons next to them copy the link ("Link copied") and open it.

### What people see by the link

- **A public artifact** opens on its own, without the Palitra interface.
- **A members-only artifact** opens in Palitra with a thin bar on top and an "Open in Palitra"
  button. Someone who is not in the project sees "You don't have access to this artifact"; they
  need to ask a project manager to add them, or sign in with another account.
- If the artifact was unpublished, the link shows "This artifact is not available"; if the link
  is wrong or the artifact was deleted, "Artifact not found".

## Update the published version

A publication serves the version you published. When the agent saves a new version, the link
keeps serving the old one, and the panel shows "Published version …, current is …".

Click "Update publication": the link starts serving the current version with the same audience
and address, and Palitra shows "The link now serves version …". If this needs the public-data
confirmation, the publish dialog opens.

To change the audience, version or address, choose "Change publication…" in the "Actions" menu.

## Unpublish

1. Choose "Unpublish" in the panel's "Actions" menu or the list's row menu.
2. Confirm with "Unpublish".

The link stops working. If ads in connected ad accounts link to the artifact, Palitra shows "Ads
link to this artifact" with a list of campaigns and ads: they will break once you unpublish. To
go ahead anyway, click "Unpublish anyway".

## Data and refresh

Dashboards show project data. Palitra keeps a snapshot of it and refreshes the snapshot by hand
or on a schedule. Artifacts with data have a "Data" tab:

- "Last updated" — when the data last refreshed successfully ("Never" if it hasn't yet);
- "Stale reason" — if the last refresh failed;
- "Auto-refresh" — how often to refresh: "Off", "Hourly", "Daily" or "Weekly".

To refresh now, click "Refresh now". While it runs, the button reads "Refreshing…".

Project managers and the organization's owner and admins choose the auto-refresh interval, for
dashboards only. The most frequent interval available depends on the plan: if an interval isn't
available on yours, Palitra offers to change the plan.

<Screenshot id="artifacts-data" alt="The Data tab with the last update time, the auto-refresh choice and the Refresh now button" />

Why data can go stale:

| Reason | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "The last refresh failed because the data query returned an error." | Ask the agent in chat to check the dashboard's query. |
| "The last refresh took too long and was stopped." | Ask the agent to narrow the period or simplify the dashboard. |
| "The data source was unavailable during the last refresh." | Click "Refresh now" again later. |
| "The refreshed data is too large to store." | Ask the agent to cut down the data. |

Errors from a manual refresh: "The data source isn't available right now.", "The data source took
too long to respond.", "Too many refresh requests." — wait and try again.

If the last refresh of a public artifact failed, the panel says "The public page shows no data
until a refresh succeeds." The same warning can appear right after publishing: "Published, but
the public page has no data yet".

## Forms

If a page has a form, the "Forms" tab sets where submissions go:

1. Fill in "Email", "Webhook URL" or both.
2. Click "Save".

The first time you save a webhook, Palitra shows a "Webhook secret" dialog: use the secret to
verify request signatures. "You won't be able to see this secret again. Copy it now." Click "Copy
secret", then "Done". "Rotate secret" issues a new one.

To stop receiving submissions, click "Remove" and confirm. The bottom of the tab shows
"Deliveries, last 24 hours": "Delivered", "Pending", "Discarded".

Project managers and the organization's owner and admins can change destinations. Everyone else
sees them read-only.

## Delete an artifact

1. Choose "Delete" in the panel's "Actions" menu or the list's row menu.
2. Confirm with "Delete" in the "Delete this artifact?" dialog.

The artifact and all its versions are deleted for good, and its link stops working. If ads link
to the artifact, Palitra lists them first; to go ahead, click "Delete anyway".

## Statuses and errors

**"Taken down".** Palitra has taken the artifact's publication down. The panel says "The
platform took this artifact down. Publishing is disabled; you can still delete it." Only Palitra
support can restore the publication.

**"No access".** You don't have access to the artifact: ask a project manager for access.

**"Artifact not found or deleted".** The artifact is no longer in the project, or the link to it
is wrong.

**"Version … was not found. Showing the current version …".** The link pointed to a version that
no longer exists.

Errors when publishing:

- "This domain isn't active yet. Finish its DNS setup first." — finish checking the domain in
  [project settings](/project-settings/domains/).
- "This path on the domain is already taken" — pick another path.
- "Members-only artifacts can't be published on a custom domain. Publish it publicly or on its
  Palitra address."
- "The current custom address will be replaced by the Palitra address." — the domain the artifact
  was published on no longer works; saving moves the publication to the Palitra address.
